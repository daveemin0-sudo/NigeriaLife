Character base meshes (NigeriaLife)

human_male.glb, human_female.glb
  Bodies, faces, eyes, hair (Hair_Buzzed / Hair_Buns) and skin textures:
    Quaternius, Universal Base Characters (Standard, free version), Superhero_Male / Superhero_Female
    https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
  Animation clips (idle, walk, run, sprint, sit, sit_talk, sit_enter, sit_exit, drive, talk, dance, interact,
  pickup, push, walk_formal, kneel, crouch_walk, fix, fall):
    Quaternius, Universal Animation Library (Standard), renamed to the game's clip names
    https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), both packs
  Built by tools/characters/build_ubc.mjs: the body split into pieces by what covers them, clothing
  shells cut from the body itself, WebP textures, everyday colours (the game tints them per player).
