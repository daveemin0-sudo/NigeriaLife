## How it plays, in its own words

- **A place is a menu.** "Actions are sped up: a meal takes seconds." A venue has stations (dance floor, bar, DJ booth); a station lists timed actions with a price and a change to needs. A trip across town "takes a few seconds".
- **Work.** One 60-minute shift a day on the career's work days, between 6 AM and 10 PM. The player picks a work style (Do Your Work, Work Like Jaguda, Gist with Colleagues, Suck Up to Oga, Take Am Easy) and answers pop-up tasks within 2 minutes; 4 won gives +15%. Ranks with grades I to IV; a new rank needs a skill level, Rep and days served; the top post needs a board interview. Leaving early pays the share worked (minimum 25% after the first 10%). "Go automatically" sends the Sim to work. Nobody is fired.
- **Starting.** Five steps: Look, Personality (exactly 2 of 10 traits), Dream (5), Birth lottery (1 in 5 Nepo Baby with ₦500,000, a car and a flat; otherwise a ₦60,000 loan), Home (Mushin ₦16,000 cash and ₦2,400 a week; Yaba ₦36,000 and ₦6,000). A 7-step tour follows. An account needs a name, username, password, email and date of birth.
- **Needs.** Hunger falls 5.5% an hour, bladder 7%, energy 4%. Up to 6 queued actions. Feelings add to or take from mood ("Sapa Don Hold Me", "Soft Life", "Landlord Palava"). Skills fade when unused.
- **Love and family.** Date up to 4 players; closeness 40 to ask; ring ₦150,000; wedding ₦500,000; parties ₦300,000 (10 guests), ₦2,000,000 (40), ₦10,000,000 (100) at a chosen event centre; prenup; divorce settlement, Live Court. Up to 3 children with milestones over real days; a nanny.
- **Visits.** Knock on a player's house, be let in, walk, sit, watch TV, house chat; up to 100 guests.
- **Groups.** Up to 100 members, plans with a place and a time, pinned messages.
- **Travel.** Lagos to Abuja ₦150,000 economy to ₦1,350,000 first; own jet ₦3m to ₦9m; coaches and night buses by named companies. In town: keke ₦100 + ₦5/km, cab ₦300 + ₦15/km.
- **Clubs.** Spraying (₦500 to ₦1m, notes others pick up), song requests, a DJ who is a player, VIP tables; very rich players get ₦10m to ₦1bn options. Net worth of ₦1bn makes most prices 10 times higher.
- **Real money.** Packs from ₦1,000 (₦50,000 game money) to ₦20,000 (₦1,600,000), by Flutterwave.
- **Weak phones.** A place card stands in when 3D will not load; "safe mode" opens the map without 3D.
- **Also in the handbook (titles only read):** insurance, EFCC, jail and bail, crime, savings and bonds, a property fund, land and sea plots, billboards, a rich list, government and elections, a stadium, table games and a games developer platform, a gist feed, music promotion, a gem hunt, home extensions and partitions, a decorator for hire, account locks, moderation and a selling filter.

## What this means for us

1. **Breadth is their strength, and it is menu breadth.** 179 places and a thousand actions, but each is a timed row on a card. Our answer stays the one in the blueprint: fewer places, every one a room where the action is acted out. We should not try to match the count by launch.
2. **Abuja is their biggest city (82 places).** Ours is our thinnest. This moves "own rooms for Abuja and Port Harcourt" up.
3. **Their work loop is a 60-minute timer with pop-ups.** Ours is tasks acted out at the workplace. Keep ours, and add what theirs has that ours lacks: work days, a choice of how to work, ranks tied to skills, leaving early for part pay.
4. **Their first five minutes are much richer than ours:** traits, a dream, a birth lottery and a choice of first home with different cash. Ours is now a name, a look and a five-step guide.
5. **Player-to-player life is where they are furthest ahead:** dating, marriage with real costs, children, house visits, groups with plans. We have marriage and online presence; visits and groups are in the blueprint's B list.
6. **They sell game money.** We do not. This is a decision for the owner, not for me.
7. **They ship a weak-phone fallback.** We have nothing like it and have not measured a cheap phone.

To verify by play (needs the owner's signed-in session; I have no browser): how a place feels inside, how long loading takes on a phone, whether work is truly a timer, and how many players are on at once.
